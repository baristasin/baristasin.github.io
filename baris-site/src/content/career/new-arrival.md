A serious game we developed for the second-semester OpenLab at CGL. It helps international students in Germany learn everyday procedures such as address registration, visa procedures and apartment hunting in an entertaining way.

The game turns bureaucratic stress into a zombie outbreak. Players defend Cologne Cathedral against bureaucratic zombies with weapons like a stamp, a copy machine, a pretzel and a bratwurst for close combat.
