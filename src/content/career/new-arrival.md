The game runs on a morning and night cycle.

First, the super fun(!) games we play in the mornings:

**Anmeldung** *(n.)* — The sacred act of informing a German city that you exist. Anmeldung requires proof of address. Proof of address requires an Anmeldung <3

**Wohnungssuche** *(n.)* — A competitive sport. 400 applicants, one 14 m² room, no window, listed as "charming". You are not going to get it. Apply anyway...

**Aufenthaltstitel** *(n.)* — A small plastic card that decides your entire future, issued by an office with no appointments available until 2036 :')

All morning, a "Sanity" bar keeps dropping and makes every game harder to play. Eventually, as every victim of bureaucracy knows, sanity hits zero and night falls: a nightmare called "Minijob".

At night you defend Cologne Cathedral against bureaucratic zombies. Luckily we have very effective weapons: a staple gun, a pretzel boomerang, a bratwurst sword and a cuckoo clock. And of course auto turrets: a copy machine, a Pfand machine, a döner and traffic lights.
