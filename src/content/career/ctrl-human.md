CTRL_HUMAN is an open field of experimentation between human, machine and medium, a place where gaming, performance and digital systems become one shared practice. Together with the Bildungswerk Brotfabrik Bonn, it builds an open infrastructure for creative technologies: workshops, game jams, performances and collective experiments, run as an ongoing process rather than as single events. Technology is not explained here but used, tested, broken and developed further together.

The project is funded by the Innovationsfonds of the Bezirksregierung Köln (under the WbG) and by the Ministry of Culture and Science of North Rhine-Westphalia.
