A Shakespeare adaptation. If you are at an OpenLab and players keep coming to your game one after another, why not have each of them write a love letter to the next? Better still, the game doesn't stay digital: each letter is delivered anonymously to its addressee in the real world, so they go home with a memory.

Players picked the words their letters would contain and the feelings they would carry. Dozens of letters from dozens of anonymous poets reached their owners. Some discovered a great talent for poetry, while others chose to draw instead and made our LOVEAI system malfunction…
